## App name (max 30)
Shop Payment Tracker

## Short description (max 80)
Keep a private ledger of the payments you receive, from the SMS you choose.

## Full description (max 4000)
Shop Payment Tracker turns the payment messages you already get into a simple, private ledger for your shop.

Open the app and see how much money you received today, how many payments, and your all-time total. Every payment is listed by day, with who paid, which account it came from, the time and the amount.

YOU CHOOSE WHAT IS READ
When you set up, the app shows the senders who texted your phone in the last 7 days. Tick only your bank or wallet. Messages from every other sender are never saved. You can add or remove accounts at any time, and when you remove one you decide whether to keep or delete its history.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Everything stays on your phone. There is no server and no account.
- No ads and no tracking.
- You can delete all your data in Settings whenever you want.

OPTIONAL VOICE ANNOUNCEMENTS
Turn on voice and the app announces each new payment aloud, in English and then in Nepali, so you can hear it even when you are busy at the counter. Voice is off until you switch it on, and Quiet hours mutes it without stopping the ledger.

ENGLISH AND NEPALI
The whole app works in English or Nepali. Change the language in Settings at any time.

HOW IT WORKS
The app asks for permission to read SMS. It uses it to find received-payment messages from the senders you chose, and to list recent senders once while you set up. It is not your messaging app: your normal messaging app keeps working, and Shop Payment Tracker never sends or deletes a message.

Please note: payment detection looks for common words such as "credited" and "received" next to an amount (NPR or Rs). Messages written in an unusual way may not be recognised.
