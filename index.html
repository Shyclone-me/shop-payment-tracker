Shop Payment Tracker: Privacy Policy

App: Shop Payment Tracker (Android package com.shopvoice.smsassistant)
Developer: 5hyclone
Contact: 5hycl0n3@gmail.com
Effective date: 2026/20/10

In short: the app reads payment text messages only from the senders you choose and keeps them on your phone. It has no server, no account, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive, collect, sell or share your messages or any other data.

1. What the app does

Shop Payment Tracker helps shopkeepers keep a ledger of money received. It looks for payment-received text messages (SMS) from the bank or wallet accounts that you select, saves each confirmed payment in a ledger on your phone, and shows daily and all-time totals. You can optionally turn on a spoken announcement of each new payment.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy the app uses it
Receive SMS (RECEIVE_SMS)To be notified when a new text message arrives, so a new payment can be recorded and, if you turned voice on, announced.
Read SMS (READ_SMS)(a) When you set up the app or press Add account, the app looks through the last 7 days of messages once to list the senders, so you can pick which ones are your payment senders. This list is kept only in memory while the screen is open and is then discarded. (b) When you add a sender, the app reads today’s payment messages from that sender so your ledger is complete for today.

The app is not your default messaging app. It does not send, delete or change any message. Your normal messaging app keeps working as before.

3. What is stored on your phone

  • Ledger entries for payments from the senders you chose: the amount, the sender name, the time, the text of that payment message and, when the message contains one, the payer’s name.
  • Your choices: the list of senders you track, your shop name, your language and your voice settings.
  • A short-lived waiting list of newly received payments that is cleared as soon as the app has saved them to the ledger.

Messages from senders you did not choose, and messages that are not received-payment messages (such as one-time passwords, offers, balance alerts or money you sent), are not saved.

4. What we do not do

  • The app has no server and does not send your data anywhere. It is built without the Android internet permission.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party data collection.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for any purpose other than showing your own ledger to you.

5. Your control and deletion

  • Remove a sender: Settings, Tracked accounts. You choose whether to keep or delete that sender’s ledger entries.
  • Delete everything: Settings, Delete data. This erases the ledger, tracked senders, shop name and settings from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the phone.
  • Withdraw permission: you can turn off SMS access at any time in your phone’s Settings, Apps, Permissions.

6. Storage and security

Data is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone and is not included in Android cloud backups. The app does not add its own encryption on top of Android’s protection of private app storage. If you lose your phone or it is rooted or unlocked by someone else, they may be able to see the data. Use a screen lock.

7. Voice announcements

Spoken announcements use your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The text spoken is only the amount received. Your phone’s speech engine is provided by your device maker or Google and is governed by its own terms.

8. Children

The app is for shop owners and is not directed at children.
